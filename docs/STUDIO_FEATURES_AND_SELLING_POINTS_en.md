# Maxshot Studio: Product Highlights and Features (Marketing Brief)

## 1. Positioning

**Create images and videos from text or images in one place. Switch between 20+ leading models, with prices you can see up front.**

Chinese: 在同一个入口里，用文字或图片生成图片和视频。20 多个主流模型自由切换，价格公开透明。

---

## 2. Core Selling Points

| # | Selling point | What to say |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Four ways to create, one place** | Text to image, image to image, text to video, and image to video, with no jumping between tools |
| 2 | **25 leading models, one switch away** | 15 image models and 10 video models, including Seedream, FLUX, Qwen Image, Recraft, Grok Imagine, Riverflow, Veo 3.1, Seedance, Wan, and Kling |
| 3 | **See the price before you generate** | The system gives a firm quote for your exact selection before you submit |
| 4 | **Videos render in the background** | Submit and leave. Your video stays in your library as "Pending" and updates automatically when it is done |
| 5 | **Precise control** | First and last frame, up to 9 reference images, a random seed, and per-model aspect ratio, resolution, and duration options |
| 6 | **High-end output** | Selected models offer 4K resolution, videos up to 30 seconds, and up to 10 images per request |
| 7 | **Bring your own soundtrack** | Supported video models accept your own audio (WAV or MP3) as the soundtrack |
| 8 | **A library you can build on** | Filter your creations by type and status, and use "Try this" to reload a prompt and its references. Turn any image into a video in one step |
| 9 | **Every model's price is public** | The Pricing page lists complete prices for text, image, and video models, and stays up to date |
| 10 | **One Maxshot account** | Chat and Studio share one account, with Google, GitHub, or wallet login |

## 3. Features

### Generate

| Feature | Details |
|---|---|
| Generation paths | Text to image, image to image, text to video, image to video |
| Model choice | Separate model lists for images and videos |
| Aspect ratio | Per model: 6 to 18 options for image models, 2 to 7 for video models |
| Resolution | Per model: 768, 1K, 1.5K, 2K, and 4K for images; 480p, 720p, 1080p, and 4K for video |
| Video duration | Per model: Veo 4–8 seconds, Seedance 4–15 seconds (up to 30 seconds on 2.5), Kling 3–15 seconds, Wan 3.0 2–30 seconds |
| Prompt | Up to 5,000 characters with a live counter, plus one-click prompt optimization |
| Reference images | Up to 9, in JPEG, PNG, or WebP, up to 32 MB each. Upload them or pick from your own creations. Reorder, remove, and preview |
| First and last frame | Every video model accepts a first frame and a last frame, so you control how a clip opens and closes |
| Sound | Generate sound, or upload your own soundtrack (WAV or MP3, up to 32 MB) |
| Random seed | Supported by most models, for reproducing and refining results |
| More options | Per model: quality (Grok), output format and background (Riverflow), negative prompt (Veo, Kling, Wan 2.7), watermark (Seedance) |
| Multiple images per request | Seedream 4.5 up to 10, Recraft and Qwen Image up to 6, Seedream 5.0 Lite up to 4 |
| Quote before you generate | A firm price for your exact selection before you submit |

### While it generates

| Feature | Details |
|---|---|
| Status | Pending, Generated, Failed |
| Background generation | Leave the page at any time without interrupting generation |
| Concurrency | Up to 3 videos can generate at once |
| Content safety | A clear message appears when a request is blocked by a safety check |

### My Creations

| Feature | Details |
|---|---|
| Filters | By type (All, Images, Videos) and status (All, Generated, Pending, Failed) |
| Management | View details, download, delete (with confirmation), and load more |
| Image to video | Send any image creation straight into image-to-video |
| Try this | Reload a creation's prompt and references into the composer, adjust, and generate again |

### Pricing page

| Feature | Details |
|---|---|
| Coverage | Text (400+), image (60+), and video (30) models, refreshed every 5 minutes |
| Price display | Credits alongside USD, with a "from" price, an input price, and an output price |
| Price breakdown | By mode (text to video, image to video, video to video), with or without audio, resolution, minimum charge, and more |
| Special pricing | Long-prompt tiers, scheduled rates by time window, cache prices, and other charges |
| Providers | Each model's providers and their prices |
| Model details | Context length, max output, reasoning support, knowledge cutoff, and release date. Image and video models also show resolutions, aspect ratios, reference-image limits, and first and last frame support |
| Extras | Search models and copy a model ID in one click |

## 7. Messaging Guidelines

- **Model counts and versions change with providers.** Say "20+ models," or name only the model families. Do not promise that a specific version will always be available.
- **4K, 30-second videos, multiple images per request, and soundtrack upload are model-specific.** Always say "selected models."
- **Support differs by model.** Aspect ratios, resolutions, durations, and reference-image limits vary. For example, Veo supports only 16:9 and 9:16, and Recraft V4.1 Flash does not accept reference images.
- **The quote shown before you generate is the price that applies.** Do not quote specific amounts in copy. The Pricing page lists more models than Studio offers, so do not imply that every model on the Pricing page can be used in Studio.
- **Prompt optimization can use Credits.** Do not describe it as free.
- **These features are not available. Do not promote them:** inpainting, background removal, upscaling, edit-by-description, video extension, style presets, favorites, and sorting by views. Follow-up editing is limited to turning an image into a video.
- **These points are not confirmed. Do not make promises about them:** whether failed generations are charged, and the sharing rules for results on the Materials page.
